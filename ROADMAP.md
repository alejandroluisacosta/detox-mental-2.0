🧭 Roadmap

Detox Mental evolves in phases that mirror its own philosophy — awareness first, then dialogue, then integration.

Each major version marks a conceptual shift, not just a technical one.

### v1.0 — The Core MVP

State: ✅ Released

The foundation of Detox Mental: the main article, the course, and the session–unblocking system.
Users can read, reflect, and unlock their way through the essential material — the “static mind” version of the app.

### v1.1 — The Exercises Update

State: ✅ Released

Adds guided exercises to each session, prompting real introspection and emotional work.
This is where Detox Mental starts moving from content to practice.

### v2.0 — Conversational Detox

State: 🧠 Planned

The article becomes interactive.
An AI-powered chat replaces the static introduction, guiding users dynamically through their own mental Detox journey.
This is the shift from reading Detox Mental to thinking with Detox Mental.

### v2.5 — Identity Layer

State: 🔒 Planned

User accounts, progress tracking, and personalization.
The system begins to remember — adapting the experience to each user’s rhythm and path.

### v3.0 — Monetization Layer

State: 💳 Planned

Stripe integration and membership options.
The project evolves from a self-contained prototype into a sustainable product.

### Vision:
Detox Mental is not just a mental health tool. It’s an ongoing experiment in self-observation and human–machine dialogue — how technology can mirror our thinking rather than distract from it.
