# Education i18n glossary (ES → EN)

Spanish is the source language. Do not change Spanish wording or meaning.
English is the default UI language. Long-form English should sound warm, calm,
and second-person, in a wellness register — not clinical, not marketing-loud.

Health disclaimers, crisis notes, and therapy recommendations must keep their
meaning exactly. Softening or omitting a warning is not a valid translation.

Product names stay as written in both languages unless this table says otherwise.

## The five steps

Keep this order and force. "Mata" is blunt in Spanish; English should stay
direct, not euphemistic.

| # | Spanish | English | Short gloss |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Toma distancia | Step back | You are not your mind. |
| 2 | Reconoce | Recognize | Thoughts cannot harm you if you do not feed them. |
| 3 | Comprende | Understand | Learn what the thought is for, then starve it of attention. |
| 4 | Ármate | Arm yourself | Stillness as the way to steer attention. |
| 5 | Mata | Kill | Practice until tormenting thoughts die of neglect. |

## Style for English long-form

- Address the reader as **you**.
- Keep the Spanish mix of story, blunt honesty, and care.
- Inclusive Spanish (tú / amigo/a / mismo/a) becomes natural English "you" /
  "friend" / "yourself" unless a gendered meaning is required.
- Do not add diagnosis language, scores, or clinical framing.
- Keep humor when the Spanish is wry; do not make it jokey.
- Book titles: use the common English title when one exists
  (*The Power of Now*, *A New Earth*, *The Subtle Art of Not Giving a F\*ck*).

## Health and disclaimer rule

If the Spanish warns the reader, tells them to stop, or points them to a
mental-health professional, the English must say the same thing with the same
strength. Examples already in the source:

- Sensitive-reader note at the start of the theory article
- Session 2 exercise note about stopping if sensations become overwhelming
- Theory notes that meditation is not therapy and that professional help is
  the main recommendation
- Instructions closing line about seeing a professional at least once a month
